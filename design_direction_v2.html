Why this, specifically: the knot line-drawing in the header and echoed faintly in the Matches tab icon is the gathbandhan — the literal knot tied between bride and groom's clothing during the ceremony. It replaces the generic heart icon every dating/matrimony clone defaults to, and gives the app one motif that's actually its own rather than borrowed from Tinder.

The scalloped header edge is drawn from a thali or rangoli border, not a rounded rectangle with a gradient — it's the one place I spent the "boldness" the brief calls for, so everything else stays quiet.

The card shape (two square corners, two rounded) and the paper-tag "Express interest" label — cut like a hand-tied gift tag rather than a pill button — read as considered, not templated, because they're asymmetric instead of the identical-radius-everywhere default.

Colour: deep ink-brown (#241A10) + turmeric gold (#C89A4A) + a single terracotta-red accent (#B4472A) for action items — closer to an actual wedding card's ink and foil than the pink-forward palette every competing app already uses.

If this reads as the right level of distinctive, I'll build the whole app on it. If it's still not landing, tell me what's off — too ornate, too dark, wrong reference entirely — and I'll go again.
